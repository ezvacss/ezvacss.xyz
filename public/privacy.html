Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect basic URL redirection records, timestamps, truncated IP addresses, and User Agent headers to generate click analytics and prevent database abuse. If you register an account, we store your username and securely hashed password.

2. How We Use Data

Your click logs are only visible to the owner of the shortened URL. We use this data to render your personal statistics dashboard (clicks, devices, actions) and to monitor for malicious redirection activity.

3. Third Parties

ezvacss.xyz does not share, rent, or sell user click metrics or personal accounts to any third-party marketing networks or data brokers.

4. Security

Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing protocols. We enforce HTTPS security controls via Cloudflare to protect data in transit.

5. Cookies

We only use a single, strictly necessary cookie (session_id) for logged-in users to manage secure login sessions. We do not use any third-party tracking, advertising, or marketing cookies.
