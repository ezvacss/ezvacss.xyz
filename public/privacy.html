Privacy Policy.

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we never touch.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a short link or redirect through ezvacss.xyz, our servers log minimal standard network traffic metadata required to process the request. This includes:

- Anonymized IP address for geo-location routing.
- User-Agent header (browser type and device OS category).
- Referrer headers.
- Timestamp of click event.

2. How We Use Data

Click logs are processed solely to aggregate total redirection statistics and populate your private user dashboard. We do not sell user data to third-party advertisers.

3. Cookies

We use lightweight session cookies strictly to maintain user authentication status on logged-in sessions.
